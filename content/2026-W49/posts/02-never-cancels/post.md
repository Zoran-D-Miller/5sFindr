## Alt text (one per slide)

1. Cover: Run a 5s that never cancels. 6 habits from organisers who play every week.
2. 01. Same day, same time: a fixed slot becomes a habit, and habits don't cancel.
3. 02. Invite 12 for 10 spots: two always drop. Plan for it.
4. 03. Keep a waiting list: names 11 and 12 move up the second someone pulls out.
5. 04. Court money up front: collect before kick-off, not after.
6. 05. One link for subs: share it outside the group when you're short.
7. 06. Notice who shows up: your regulars are gold. Ask them first next week.
