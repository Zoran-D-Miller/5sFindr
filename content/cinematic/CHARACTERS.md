# Characters: fictional, AI-generated, never presented as users

These three exist only in the films. They have no names on screen, no quotes, no "member" status, and they're never in captions as people who use 5sFindr. The names below are internal labels to keep prompts consistent.

**How consistency works with free tools:**
1. Generate **one reference still** per character from the prompt below (Flow's image tool or Firefly image). Make 4, pick 1, and keep it in `cinematic/refs/`.
2. Start every shot from that still (frames-to-video / reference / image-to-video) **and** repeat the wardrobe anchors word for word in the shot prompt.
3. Keep faces half-lit, three-quarter from behind, or in silhouette. Hands, boots, backs and walk carry the story.
4. If a take drifts (different jacket, different hair), throw it away. Never patch a face.

## NIA (the newcomer)

- **Role in the films:** new to the city, arrives alone, gets pulled in. The "you" of the story.
- **Look:** woman, mid-20s, dark brown skin, short black twists tied back, slim athletic build.
- **Wardrobe anchors (always):** oversized **dark olive half-zip windbreaker**, black training shorts, **white crew socks**, black boots with **one pitch-green lace** (left boot), small silver hoop earrings.
- **Never:** logos, club crests, text on clothing, jewellery other than the hoops.

**Reference still prompt:**
> Full-body three-quarter-back portrait of a woman in her mid-20s with dark brown skin and short black twists tied back, slim athletic build, wearing an oversized dark olive half-zip windbreaker, black training shorts, white crew socks and black football boots with one bright green lace on the left boot, small silver hoop earrings, standing on a dark street at blue hour in Cape Town with Lion's Head in soft silhouette behind, cool streetlight rim light, 35mm film, fine grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos.

## LUCA (the traveller)

- **Role:** another stranger who shows up. Proof that "everyone came alone once".
- **Look:** man, late 20s, olive skin, dark curly hair, short beard.
- **Wardrobe anchors:** **black beanie**, **navy-and-white hooped football shirt with no crest**, grey joggers, small black backpack with one strap worn.
- **Never:** real club shirts, sponsor logos.

**Reference still prompt:**
> Three-quarter portrait of a man in his late 20s with olive skin, dark curly hair under a black beanie and a short beard, wearing a navy-and-white hooped football shirt with no crest or logo, grey joggers, a small black backpack on one shoulder, standing beside a chain-link fence at night with floodlight glow behind him, haze, 35mm film, fine grain, no text, no logos.

## THE CAPTAIN (the organiser)

- **Role:** runs the game. Calm, welcoming, holds the bibs. Stands for every real organiser, never a real one.
- **Look:** man, mid-30s, medium-brown skin, close-cropped hair, stocky build.
- **Wardrobe anchors:** black hoodie under a **plain pitch-green training bib**, **whistle on a black cord**, carries a **mesh bag of footballs** and a stack of black bibs.
- **Never:** referee kit, real-venue staff uniform.

**Reference still prompt:**
> Portrait of a stocky man in his mid-30s with medium-brown skin and close-cropped hair, wearing a black hoodie under a plain bright green training bib with no print, a whistle on a black cord around his neck, holding a mesh bag of footballs, standing on an artificial grass five-a-side pitch at night under cool white floodlights with haze, warm half smile, 35mm film, fine grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos.

## Global style block (append to every shot prompt)

> Shot on 35mm film at night, cool white stadium floodlights with light haze, deep blacks and soft crushed shadows, fine film grain with gentle halation, muted colour with a single vivid pitch-green accent, anamorphic flare, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, documentary realism, Cape Town. Vertical 9:16. No on-screen text, no logos, no brand names, no readable phone screens.

**Negative / avoid** (where the tool has a field for it): text, captions, subtitles, logos, brand names, readable phone interface, watermarks, extra fingers, warped hands, distorted faces, crowd, stadium seating, rain.
