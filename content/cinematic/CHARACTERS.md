# Cast: fictional, AI-generated, faceless, never presented as users

**Rules (Zoran, 22 Nov 2026), on top of README §3:**
- **No faces, ever.** Every character is filmed from behind, in silhouette, from the neck down, or as hands and boots only. If a take shows a face or readable eyes, throw it away.
- **Men's films are men-only.** No mixed-gender scenes. Women appear only in future **women-only game** films, in loose, full-length, modest clothing, also faceless (to be written when that feature exists).
- **Men's kit covers navel to knee:** full-length joggers or track pants, or knee-length shorts over long tights. Never shorts above the knee.
- **Never:** jewellery, logos, club crests, sponsor kit, alcohol, betting signs, bars.

The names below are internal labels to keep prompts consistent. They are never shown on screen, never quoted, never called members or users.

**How consistency works with free tools:**
1. Make **one reference still** per character from the prompt below (Google Flow's image tool). Make 4, pick 1, and save it in `cinematic/refs/`.
2. Start every shot from that still (Flow: **Frames to video** or **Ingredients**), **and** paste the wardrobe anchors word for word into the shot prompt.
3. Hands, boots, backs, the walk and the phone glow carry the story.
4. If a take drifts (other jacket, other colour lace, a face appears), throw it away. Never patch.

## THE NEWCOMER (the "you" of every film)

- **Role:** new in Cape Town, arrives alone, gets pulled into a game, leaves with a crew.
- **Seen as:** back, shoulders, hands, boots. Medium-brown skin on the hands. Short dark hair from behind.
- **Wardrobe anchors (always, word for word):** **dark olive half-zip windbreaker**, **full-length grey joggers**, **black football boots with one bright green lace on the left boot**, **small black backpack worn on one shoulder**.

**Reference still prompt:**
> Full-body back view of a young man in his late twenties with short dark hair, seen from behind so his face is not visible, wearing a dark olive half-zip windbreaker, full-length grey joggers, black football boots with one bright green lace on the left boot, and a small black backpack on one shoulder, standing on a quiet Cape Town street at blue hour with Lion's Head in soft silhouette ahead of him, cool streetlight rim light, 35mm film, fine grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos.

## THE CAPTAIN (the organiser)

- **Role:** runs the game, calm, welcoming. Stands for every real organiser, never a real one.
- **Seen as:** silhouette or from behind, stocky build, a raised hand.
- **Wardrobe anchors:** **black hoodie under a plain bright green training bib**, **whistle on a black cord**, **black full-length track pants**, carries a **mesh bag of footballs**.

**Reference still prompt:**
> Back view of a stocky man in his mid-thirties, face not visible, wearing a black hoodie under a plain bright green training bib with no print, a whistle on a black cord, black full-length track pants, holding a mesh bag of white footballs, standing on an artificial-grass five-a-side pitch at night under cool white floodlights with haze, strong backlight so he reads as a silhouette, 35mm film, fine grain, no text, no logos.

## THE CREW (background players)

- 8 men in plain dark tops and full-length training pants, always backlit silhouettes or out of focus. No faces, no numbers, no crests.

## Global style block (append to every shot prompt)

> Shot on 35mm film at night, cool white floodlights with light haze, deep blacks and soft crushed shadows, fine film grain with gentle halation, muted colour with a single vivid pitch-green accent, anamorphic flare, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, documentary realism, Cape Town. Vertical 9:16. Faces are never visible: people are seen from behind, in backlit silhouette, or cropped below the shoulders. All men wear full-length trousers or joggers. No on-screen text, no logos, no brand names, no readable phone screens.

**Negative / avoid** (where the tool has a field for it): faces, visible eyes, close-up of a person's face, women, bare knees, short shorts, text, captions, subtitles, logos, brand names, readable phone interface, alcohol, bar, betting, watermarks added by editing, extra fingers, warped hands, crowd, stadium seating, rain.
