## The idea

A kit-room ritual. A stencil, a can of pitch-green spray paint, a black bib. Number by number, a crew is being made, and you can't buy your way in. The number appears in our own type at the end, and it isn't faked by the AI.

- **Identity and belonging:** a number is a place in a group. Founding numbers are earned by joining early, never sold (layer 3 of "Open door, inner circle").
- **Real scarcity:** a fixed, published count of founding numbers. The film never says "almost gone" unless the live count says so.
- **Curiosity gap:** what are the numbers for? The plain line answers it.

**S02** (THE CAPTAIN reference)
> Medium shot in a dim concrete kit room at night: a stocky man in his mid-30s, black hoodie under a plain bright green training bib, whistle on a black cord, hangs black training bibs with freshly sprayed green numbers on metal hooks; cool floodlight spills through a high window; the bib numbers are out of focus and unreadable.

**S04** (NIA reference)

**S05**
> Wide low-angle shot from behind: six players in black training bibs walk side by side out of a tunnel onto an artificial-grass five-a-side pitch under cool white floodlights with heavy haze and anamorphic flare; silhouettes, numbers on the bibs soft and unreadable.

**S06**
> Extreme close-up: a hand presses lightly on a freshly sprayed bright green stencil digit on a black bib; wet paint glistens under floodlight; very shallow depth of field.
