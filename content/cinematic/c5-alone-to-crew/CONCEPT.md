# C5 · "Alone → Crew": new city, no squad, one link

**Runtime:** ~16 s + end card · **Shots:** 7 (6 AI, 1 real-UI insert) · **Side:** B of the weekly A/B test · **Ready:** now (no unbuilt features)

## The idea

New in Cape Town, nobody to play with. A phone buzzes. One link, one spot. He laces up, rides the taxi across the city, walks up to a floodlit pitch where a stranger raises a hand. Ball in the net. A fist bump. **Came alone. Left with a crew.**

- **Hook (frame 1):** phone buzz sound + green glow + big title "NEW CITY. NO SQUAD." Motion and text in the first second.
- **Feeling:** premium, night, almost secret, but the plain line says it's open to everyone.
- **Honest claim:** one shared link lets a stranger nearby claim a spot. True today.
- **Islamic principles:** no faces (hands, boots, backs, silhouettes), men only, full-length trousers, no music (Veo's own audio is muted by the template), nothing haram in frame. See `../README.md` §3.
- **Labels:** on-screen chip "AI-generated scenes · real app screens" + TikTok "AI-generated content" + IG/FB "AI info". The Veo watermark stays.
